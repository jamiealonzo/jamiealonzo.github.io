My Background

Every journey starts with curiosity — mine sparked through coding and the excitement of building things from the ground up. What began as a dive into software development soon evolved into a deep passion for eCommerce and creating powerful online experiences. Over the years, I’ve worked on a variety of projects from writing clean, scalable code to designing and launching custom Shopify stores that don’t just look good, but sell. My background in software development gives me a unique edge when it comes to blending functionality with design, while my eCommerce experience allows me to understand what truly drives conversions. I approach every project with strategy, creativity, and a love for problem-solving always aiming to build digital experiences that feel seamless, personal, and built to grow with the brand.
