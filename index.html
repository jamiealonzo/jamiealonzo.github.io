What I Do

Every brand has a story — your store should tell it. I create custom Shopify experiences that combine clean design, smart features, and a focus on conversions to help your business grow, connect, and stand out.

Design & launch custom Shopify stores

From concept to conversion, I build fully tailored Shopify stores that align with your brand and business goals. Each store is crafted to be both visually engaging and user-friendly.

Customize themes with Liquid, HTML, CSS & JavaScript

Need more than the standard theme settings? I dive into the code to tweak layouts, add custom features, and create a unique storefront that sets you apart.

Optimize performance, speed & mobile responsiveness

A fast, seamless experience keeps customers engaged. I fine-tune your site for speed, mobile usability, and smooth performance across all devices.

Improve UX to increase engagement and conversions

I streamline layouts, refine user flows, and design with intention all to keep visitors engaged and guide them toward checkout with ease.

Troubleshoot issues & fix bugs

Whether it’s a layout glitch or a broken function, I identify and resolve technical issues quickly so your store keeps running smoothly.

Set up apps, integrations & advanced features

From reviews to email marketing, I’ll integrate the right tools and apps to expand your store’s functionality and streamline operations.

Enhance product pages, carts, and checkout flows

I optimize the entire shopping journey with clear layouts, trust-building elements, and smooth checkout processes that reduce drop-offs and increase sales.

Offer ongoing support and training

I offer clear, personalized guidance so you feel confident managing your store plus long-term support to help you grow and adapt.
